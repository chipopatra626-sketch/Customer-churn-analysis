# Customer Churn Analysis

Simulated dataset of 40 customers, created for practice with AI assistance.

Goal
Identify patterns linked to subscription cancellations and suggest ways to improve retention.

 Summary
Customers seem to leave when they are newer, use the service less and face more problems.

Key findings
- Overall churn: 15 of 40 customers left (37.5%).
- Tenure: customers who left averaged 9.1 months, compared with 18.6 for those who stayed.
- Engagement: customers who left averaged 5.3 logins in 30 days, compared with 13.7.
- Problems: customers who left averaged 4.3 feature errors and 3.9 support contacts, compared with 1.1 and 1.9.
- Inactivity: 87% of customers inactive for 14 days or more left (13 of 15), compared with 8% of others (2 of 25).
- Plans: Basic churn was 57% (8 of 14) and Standard was 23% (5 of 22). Premium was 50%, but with only 4 customers this is not reliable.

Recommendations to test
- Find and fix the most common feature errors.
- Contact customers before they reach 14 days without logging in.
- Give new customers extra support early on.
- Follow up with customers who contact support repeatedly.

Limitations
- The data is simulated and small, so results are for practice only.
- These patterns show links, not proven causes.
- Each customer appears once, so the data cannot show changes over time.
- Inactivity may happen after a customer decides to leave. Real analysis would need activity from before the cancellation date.
